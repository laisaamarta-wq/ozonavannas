How the session goes

Five steps, no surprises

You know what happens at every moment — and you can always say if something isn't comfortable.

  1. 01

    A short talk

    We ask how you feel and whether you have any health limitations. If the session isn't right for you, we will tell you honestly.

  2. 02

    Choosing the length

    Together we choose 20 or 30 minutes and a water temperature that feels pleasant to you.

  3. 03

    The ozone bath

    Ozone is added to the warm water, so it fizzes like mineral water. You feel a light tingle of bubbles on your skin.

  4. 04

    Massage — optional

    After the bath — a massage with ozonated oil. Warmth and massage help the muscles relax.

  5. 05

    No rush

    There is no need to hurry afterwards. During a course you note how you feel on your Recovery card.

What you may feel

  1. During the session

  2. Right after

    Often — a lighter body and a calmer head.

  3. During a course

    Recovery becomes a habit, and you see how you feel on your Recovery card.

FAQ

Common questions

Before your first visit

The session is not for everyone

When booking, tell us about your health — for example pregnancy, chronic conditions, recent surgery or how you feel that day. If the session isn't right for you, we will say so. If in doubt, please talk to a doctor first.

What is an ozone bath?

A wellness procedure: you lie in warm water to which ozone is added, so the water fizzes like mineral water. The session is run by a certified specialist.

What should I choose for my first visit?

We suggest the 45-minute session — the bath with massage. If you are short on time, start with a 20- or 30-minute bath.

How often should I come?

For regular recovery we suggest a course: 10 sessions, 2–3 times a week, for about 4–5 weeks.

Can I combine it with training?

Yes. Tell the specialist about your training load and he will adjust the length of the session.

Is it a medical service?

No. It is a wellness procedure and does not replace a doctor's advice, diagnosis or treatment.
