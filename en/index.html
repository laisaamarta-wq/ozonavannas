Who are ozone baths for?

Ozone baths are recommended for increased fatigue, stress, swelling and reduced adaptive resources of the body, as well as during recovery periods after physical load.

  3. Relieves fatigue and tension after physical activity.

Process

How it works

Five phases — from a gentle initial stimulus to deep recovery.

  1. 01/05

    Gentle oxidative stimulus

    Ozonated water creates a brief physiological stimulus for the skin and capillaries.

  2. 02/05

    Receptor and nervous system activation

    The peripheral nervous system and autonomic regulation engage.

  3. 03/05

    Eustress — beneficial stress

    The body receives a controlled signal toward adaptation, not toward defence or a panic response.

  4. 04/05

    Brief sympathetic activation

    A moderate release of catecholamines (hormones and neurotransmitters) supports clarity, tone and concentration.

  5. 05/05

    Recovery phase

    After activation, the body enters deep relaxation, restoration and a rebalancing of systems.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

01Does it stimulate or relax?

First — a gentle activation, then a phase of deep recovery. The procedure works in both directions: it tones, and then it relaxes.

02Will the nervous system be «overloaded»?

No. The stimulus is moderate and controlled — it is a eustress that fits within the body's natural adaptation response.

03How many sessions do I need?

The first effect is felt after a single session. For a lasting result, a short course is usually recommended.

04Are there any contraindications?

Yes, there are individual limits. Please share them in advance — the specialist will adapt the session.

05Can it be combined with training / massage / therapy?

Yes. An ozone bath combines well with physical activity, massage and supportive therapy.
